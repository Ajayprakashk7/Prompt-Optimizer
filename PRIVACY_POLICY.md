# Privacy Policy for Prompt Optimizer

**Last Updated: July 2025**

## Overview

Prompt Optimizer is a Chrome extension that helps users create better prompts for AI models. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

## Data Collection and Usage

### What We Collect
- **Prompts and Content**: The prompts you create and optimize are stored locally on your device
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous usage statistics to improve the extension (optional)
- **Settings and Preferences**: Your extension settings and preferences

### What We DON'T Collect
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect names, emails, or personal identifiers
- **API Keys**: Your Google AI API key is stored locally on your device only
- **Sensitive Data**: We do not access or store sensitive personal information

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
- All your prompts, settings, and API keys are stored locally in your browser
- Data is not transmitted to our servers
- You can delete all data by uninstalling the extension

### Third-Party Services
- **Google AI API**: When you use the optimization feature, your prompts are sent to Google's Generative AI API
- **Google's Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- We do not store or log these API interactions

## Data Security

- **Encryption**: Sensitive data is encrypted in local storage
- **No Server Storage**: We do not maintain servers that store your data
- **API Key Security**: Your API key never leaves your device except for direct API calls

## Your Rights

- **Access**: You can view all stored data in the extension settings
- **Deletion**: You can delete all data by clearing extension storage or uninstalling
- **Control**: You have full control over what data is stored and used

## Third-Party Integrations

### Google Generative AI
- Used for prompt optimization functionality
- Subject to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
- Data sent directly from your browser to Google's servers

## Analytics (Optional)

If you opt-in to analytics:
- **Anonymous Usage Data**: Feature usage, performance metrics
- **No Personal Data**: No personally identifiable information
- **Opt-Out**: Can be disabled in settings at any time

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We will notify users of any material changes to this privacy policy through the extension update mechanism.

This extension complies with:

## Data Retention

- **Local Data**: Retained until you delete it or uninstall the extension
- **Analytics**: Anonymous data retained for 24 months maximum
